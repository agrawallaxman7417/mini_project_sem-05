About the festival

Now in its 18th year, Sunburn Goa 2026 returns to the red laterite cliffs of Vagator Beach for a weekend of electronic music, sea breeze and sunset views. It remains one of Asia's largest dance-music gatherings.

This edition brings together international headliners and homegrown talent across four stages, with new lighting design, sunset-timed set transitions and festival-wide art installations built for the coastline.

12+Cities with travel routes

Direct travel corridors run from Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Pune into Dabolim and MOPA airports.

Festival highlights

4 signature zones

Live music performances

Four stages — Mainstage Titan, Solaris Techno Deep and Neon Tropic Indie — with global EDM names and chart-topping Indian DJs.

EDM stages & visuals

Precision laser mapping, CO2 cryo cannons and a 360° volumetric LED canopy set against the Arabian coastline.

Food & beverage village

Over 30 Goan stalls serving prawn balchão, vindaloo wraps, sourdough pizzas and fresh coconut water.

Festival experience zones

Bungee drops, a beachside ferris wheel, a sunset flea market and seaside hammock lounges.

Designated parking

Tiered parking at Chapora Open Grounds — 1,800+ four-wheeler and 3,500 two-wheeler bays, with shuttle drop-off to Gate 2.

Transit & shuttles

Express shuttles run non-stop from Thivim Railway Station (18 km) and Manohar Airport MOPA (28 km) to the festival gate.

Nearby landmarks

Walking distance from Chapora Fort and Ozran Little Vagator Beach. Licensed scooter rentals just outside.

Event policies & guidelines

Age restriction: strictly 18+

Valid original photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport or Driver's Licence) is mandatory at Box Office redemption.

RFID wristband entry

Exchange your booking QR for an official waterproof wristband at the counters. Tampered wristbands are void.

Cancellation & refunds

Tickets are non-refundable and non-transferable. Full refunds are issued automatically only if the event is cancelled.

Prohibited items

Professional cameras, drones, outside food or alcohol, and metal bottles are not allowed. Gates close at 10:00 PM.
